## Interpretation ceiling

A transfer-localized result means the isolated harmonic-knot spike is already present in the E transfer kernel. An integrand-localized result means the raw transfer norm does not meet the frozen specificity pattern but the EE quadratic/primordial weighting does. An accumulation-localized result means the two frozen pre-integration profile metrics do not reproduce the spike although the exact sparse C_l does.

None of these classes alone establishes a CLASS bug, chooses a production precision setting, proves global convergence, promotes K1/K3/K4, or physically falsifies/validates mixed cold+warm dark matter.
